# Permission Tiers and Role Upgrades

This runbook describes the standard permission levels inside a Meridian workspace and how members move between them. IT / Access owns role definitions and processes upgrade requests; workspace Owners can manage roles directly for their own account.

## The Four Tiers

Meridian uses four roles, from least to most privileged:

- **Viewer** — read-only access to projects, dashboards, and reports the member has been shared into. Viewers cannot edit content, invite users, or change settings. This is the default for new hires and most stakeholders.
- **Editor** — everything a Viewer can do, plus creating and editing projects, tickets, and records within workspaces they belong to. Editors cannot manage billing or other members.
- **Admin** — full operational control: managing members, configuring integrations, and adjusting workspace settings. Admins do not automatically control billing unless also granted the Owner role.
- **Owner** — the highest tier, with billing authority, the ability to delete the workspace, and control over all Admins. Enterprise accounts may hold multiple Owners; Starter and Business accounts typically have one.

## Requesting an Upgrade

A member requesting more access (for example, Viewer to Editor on a specific repository or workspace) files through the IT / Access portal with a business justification and their manager named. Upgrades to Editor are approved by the workspace Admin or the requester's manager and applied within one business day. Upgrades to Admin or Owner are sensitive: they require resource-owner approval in addition to manager approval and are provisioned within two business days.

## Least Privilege and Reviews

Grants follow least privilege — members receive the lowest tier that lets them do their job. Admin and Owner assignments are reviewed quarterly, and any role unused for 90 days is flagged for downgrade. Temporary elevation for a one-off task is preferred over a permanent upgrade and auto-expires.

## Ownership

IT / Access owns tier definitions, upgrade approvals, and periodic access reviews.
