# Webinar and Recording Access

This runbook explains where Meridian Cloud, Inc. webinar recordings live, how long they are kept, and how customers obtain them. Support owns recording distribution and access questions.

## Where Recordings Live

Meridian hosts a live product webinar on the second Tuesday of every month, along with occasional feature deep-dives and onboarding sessions. Every session is recorded. Recordings are published to the Resource Center at resources.meridiancloud.com, usually within two business days of the live session, alongside the slide deck and a short summary. Each recording is tagged by topic and date so it is easy to locate later.

## Public vs Gated Content

Content access follows a simple split. Short highlight clips and general overview webinars are public and posted to Meridian's YouTube channel with no sign-in required. Full-length sessions, technical deep-dives, and anything covering roadmap or advanced configuration are gated: a viewer must sign in with a Meridian account or provide a business email to unlock the recording. This lets the team share broadly while keeping detailed material with known contacts.

## Retention

Recordings are retained in the Resource Center for 12 months from the session date, then archived and removed from public listings. Archived recordings for a specific past session can still be retrieved on request while they remain in cold storage for an additional 12 months. After that, only the summary and slides are kept.

## Requesting a Recording

Customers who cannot find a session should first check the Resource Center, since nearly everything is self-serve there. If a recording is missing, not yet posted, or has aged out of the public listing, Support retrieves it. Requests are fulfilled within one business day. For gated content, Support confirms the requester is an existing customer or a verified business contact before sharing the link, and never posts gated recordings to public channels.

## Ownership

Support owns recording distribution; the marketing team produces and publishes the sessions.
