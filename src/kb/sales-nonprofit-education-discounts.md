# Nonprofit, Education, and Student Discounts

This runbook defines Meridian Cloud, Inc. discount programs for mission-driven and academic customers. Sales owns eligibility decisions and applies approved discounts to the account.

## Programs and Discount Amounts

Meridian offers three distinct programs, each applied to standard list pricing:

- **Nonprofit — 30% off.** For registered charitable organizations operating for a public benefit. Applies to Starter and Business tiers; Enterprise nonprofit pricing is quoted separately.
- **Education — 40% off.** For accredited primary, secondary, and higher-education institutions purchasing for staff or faculty use.
- **Student — 50% off.** For individual verified students using Meridian for coursework, capped at a single Starter seat per student.

Discounts apply to the subscription line only and do not extend to add-ons, premium support, or the offline-payment processing fee. They cannot be combined with promotional offer codes.

## Eligibility Verification

All three programs require verification through SheerID, our third-party eligibility partner, before the discount is applied. Nonprofits submit their registration or tax-exempt determination. Educational institutions submit proof of accreditation and a purchasing contact on an institutional domain. Students verify current enrollment. Verification typically completes within two business days; Sales does not apply a discount on the promise of documentation still pending.

## How Discounts Are Applied

Once SheerID confirms eligibility, Sales attaches the discount to the account, and it appears as a named credit line on every future invoice for the duration of the term. The discount renews automatically with the subscription, subject to annual re-verification for education and student accounts. Nonprofit status is re-checked every two years.

## Limits and Misuse

Each organization qualifies for one program; a nonprofit school chooses either the nonprofit or education rate, not both. Accounts found to have misrepresented eligibility have the discount removed and the difference invoiced. Borderline or unusual cases are reviewed by a Sales manager before approval.

## Ownership

Sales owns discount eligibility, approval, and the relationship with SheerID.
