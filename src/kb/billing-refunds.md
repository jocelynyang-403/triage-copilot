# Refund Policy and Processing

This runbook defines when Meridian Cloud, Inc. issues refunds, how amounts are calculated, and how long money takes to land. Billing Ops owns refund processing; disputed or unauthorized-charge cases are escalated to Human Review.

## Eligibility

Annual plans are refundable within 30 days of the initial charge or renewal. After 30 days, the annual term is committed and non-refundable, though the account can still cancel to prevent the next renewal. Monthly plans (available on Starter and Business) are non-refundable once the billing period begins, because the customer retains access for the full month already paid. Enterprise refunds follow the terms in the signed order form, which supersede this policy.

## Proration Rules

Within the 30-day annual window, a customer who downgrades or cancels receives a prorated refund for unused whole months, calculated from the cancellation date. Partial months are not refunded. Seats removed mid-term are not refunded; the change applies at renewal. Any promotional discount originally applied is preserved in the refund math so the customer is neither over- nor under-credited.

## Approval Authority

Refunds up to $500 are approved directly by a Billing Ops agent. Refunds between $500 and $5,000 require Billing Ops manager sign-off. Anything above $5,000, or any goodwill refund outside policy, requires Finance approval routed through the Billing Ops manager. Suspected fraudulent or unauthorized charges are not processed as routine refunds; they go to Human Review for investigation before any money moves.

## Timelines

Once approved, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds typically post in 5 to 10 business days depending on the issuing bank. ACH and wire refunds take 7 to 10 business days. Billing Ops sends written confirmation with the refund reference at the moment the refund is submitted, not when it clears.

## Ownership

Billing Ops owns refund execution; Human Review owns dispute and fraud cases.
