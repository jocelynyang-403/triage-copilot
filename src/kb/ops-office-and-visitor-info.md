# Office Location and Visitor Information

This runbook covers the Meridian Cloud, Inc. headquarters, how visitors check in, and transit and parking options. IT / Access owns building access and badge issuance; front-desk reception assists on-site visitors.

## Location

Meridian's headquarters is at 500 Harrison Street, Suite 400, San Francisco, CA 94105. Reception is on the fourth floor and is staffed Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific Time. The building is closed on all company holidays.

## Visitor Check-In

All visitors must be pre-registered by their internal host at least one business day in advance through the IT / Access portal. On arrival, visitors check in at the fourth-floor reception kiosk, present a government-issued photo ID, and receive a temporary visitor badge that grants access only to common areas and the floor they are visiting. Hosts are notified automatically at check-in and must escort their guests beyond reception. Visitor badges expire at the end of the day and are returned to reception on departure.

## Parking

The building has no dedicated visitor parking. The nearest public garage is at Fifth and Harrison, a three-minute walk away, with hourly and daily rates. Street parking is metered and limited, so it is not recommended for visits longer than an hour. There is secure bicycle parking in the building's ground-floor room, accessible with a visitor badge.

## Public Transit

The office is well served by transit. Montgomery Street BART and Muni station is about a ten-minute walk. The Caltrain terminus at Fourth and King is roughly a fifteen-minute walk or a short Muni ride. Several Muni bus lines stop within two blocks. Transit is the recommended option given the limited parking downtown.

## Building Access for Staff

Employee and contractor badge requests, replacements for lost badges, and after-hours access are all handled by IT / Access through the standard access request workflow.

## Ownership

IT / Access owns building access; reception assists with day-of visitor logistics.
