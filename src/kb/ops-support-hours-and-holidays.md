# Support Hours, SLAs, and Holiday Coverage

This runbook defines when Meridian Cloud, Inc. support is available, the response targets by priority, and how tickets are handled during closures. Support owns coverage and the ticket queue.

## Coverage by Tier

Standard support, included with Starter and Business, operates Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time, excluding company holidays. Enterprise accounts receive the same weekday coverage for routine matters plus 24/7 coverage for urgent P0 and P1 incidents through an on-call rotation. All customers reach support through the portal at help.meridiancloud.com or by emailing support@meridiancloud.com.

## Response Targets

Response SLAs are measured from ticket receipt during covered hours:

- **P0 (critical outage):** one hour, Enterprise only, 24/7.
- **P1 (major, business-impacting):** four business hours.
- **P2 (moderate):** one business day.
- **P3 (low, cosmetic, or general question):** two business days.

These are first-response targets, not resolution times. A ticket's priority is set by Support based on impact and urgency, not by the sender's own label.

## Holiday Calendar

Meridian offices and standard support close on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (Thursday and the following Friday), and for a winter shutdown from December 24 through January 1. On these dates, standard-tier response clocks pause and resume the next business day. Enterprise 24/7 P0 and P1 coverage continues uninterrupted through every holiday.

## Tickets Filed During Closures

Tickets submitted outside covered hours or during a holiday are queued and acknowledged automatically with expected response timing. They are worked in priority order when coverage resumes, with the SLA clock starting at the next business hour. Customers with an urgent issue on an Enterprise plan should mark it P0 or P1 so it reaches the on-call engineer immediately rather than waiting in the standard queue.

## Ownership

Support owns coverage hours, SLA definitions, and the holiday schedule.
