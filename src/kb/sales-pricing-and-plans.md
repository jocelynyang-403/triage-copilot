# Pricing and Plan Tiers

This runbook is the reference for Meridian Cloud, Inc. plan tiers and pricing. Sales owns quoting, plan changes, and any negotiated terms. All list prices are in US dollars and quoted per seat per month.

## Plan Tiers

Meridian sells three tiers:

- **Starter — $12 per seat per month.** For small teams getting started. Includes core ticketing and project workspaces, standard integrations, email support, and up to 25 seats.
- **Business — $28 per seat per month.** For growing teams. Adds advanced automation, custom fields and workflows, reporting dashboards, priority support, and unlimited seats.
- **Enterprise — custom, starting at $45 per seat per month with a 50-seat minimum.** Adds SSO and SCIM provisioning, audit logs, EU data residency options, a dedicated customer success manager, 24/7 support for urgent issues, and a signed SLA.

## Annual vs Monthly Billing

Annual billing is the default and the better value: paying annually gives two months free, an effective discount of roughly 17% against the monthly rate. Monthly billing is available on Starter and Business for teams that prefer flexibility; Enterprise is annual-only. Customers can switch from monthly to annual at any time, and the change is prorated onto the next invoice.

## What Drives a Quote

Enterprise pricing depends on seat volume, contract length, and add-ons such as additional data residency or premium support. Volume discounts begin at 100 seats and increase at 250 and 500 seats. Multi-year commitments unlock further discounting. A formal quote lists seat count, tier, billing cadence, any discounts, and the total, and is valid for 30 days from issue.

## Trials

Every plan offers a 14-day free trial with no card required. Trials default to Business-tier features so prospects can evaluate the full workflow set before choosing a tier.

## Ownership

Sales owns all pricing, quotes, and plan negotiations. Complex procurement or security-review requests are handled by Solutions Engineering within Sales.
