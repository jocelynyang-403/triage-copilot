# Promotions and Offer Code Policy

This runbook governs how Meridian Cloud, Inc. promotional campaigns and offer codes work. Sales owns campaign creation, code issuance, and any exception handling.

## How Offer Codes Work

Promotional codes are issued for specific campaigns — seasonal offers, event promotions, or competitive-switch incentives. Each code carries a defined validity window with explicit start and end dates, and applies only to the tiers named in the campaign. A code is redeemed at checkout or applied by Sales to a new quote. Once redeemed, the discount holds for the term stated in the campaign (typically the first year), then the account renews at standard rates unless a new agreement is in place.

## Validity Windows

Every campaign publishes its start and end dates internally, and codes stop working automatically at the end of the window. A code tied to a marketing email, for example, is valid only through the date printed in that email. Agents should confirm the current date against the campaign window before assuming a code should work.

## Stacking Rules

Offer codes do not stack. Only one promotional discount applies per account, and promotional codes cannot be combined with nonprofit, education, or student pricing — the customer receives whichever single discount is larger, not both. Codes also do not apply to add-ons, premium support, or the offline-payment processing fee.

## Honoring Expired Offers

An expired code is not honored automatically. If a customer references an offer that lapsed recently, only a Sales manager may choose to honor it, and only within 14 days of the stated expiration, at their discretion for retention or goodwill reasons. Requests to honor offers expired longer than 14 days are declined, though the account may qualify for a current active promotion instead.

## Abuse Prevention

Codes are single-use per account. Attempts to reuse a redeemed code, or to apply multiple codes, are rejected by the billing system automatically.

## Ownership

Sales owns all promotions, codes, and expired-offer exceptions.
