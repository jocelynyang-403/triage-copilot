# Requesting Invoice Copies and Receipts

This runbook covers how customers obtain historical invoices, receipts, and payment records for Meridian Cloud, Inc. Billing Ops owns the billing archive and any manual document requests.

## Self-Serve Options

Most requests never need a human. Any workspace member with the Admin or Owner role can download invoices directly from the Billing Portal at help.meridiancloud.com under Settings, Billing, Invoice History. The portal holds every invoice and paid receipt generated since the account was created, each available as a PDF. Receipts are timestamped at payment capture and include the transaction reference, plan, seat count, and tax breakdown. Agents should first confirm whether the requester holds a billing role and point them to self-serve, since this resolves the majority of requests instantly.

## When a Manual Request Is Needed

Billing Ops handles requests that self-serve cannot cover: consolidated statements spanning a full tax year, invoices predating a company-name change, reissued documents with updated VAT or exemption details, or copies for someone without portal access. The requester must supply the account's client ID or the workspace email on file so Billing Ops can verify ownership before releasing any financial document.

## Turnaround

Standard document pulls are fulfilled within one business day. Consolidated annual statements, which require assembling and reconciling multiple invoices, take up to three business days. Requests tied to an active audit or legal deadline should be flagged as priority in the ticket subject so Billing Ops can expedite.

## Verification and Privacy

Financial documents are released only to verified account contacts. If the requester cannot be matched to a billing role or the client ID on file, the ticket is held and the requester is asked to have a current Admin or Owner confirm the request. Meridian never emails full invoice PDFs to unverified addresses.

## Ownership

Billing Ops is the sole owner of the invoice archive and all reissue requests.
