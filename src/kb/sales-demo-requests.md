# Demo Request Handling

This runbook describes how Meridian Cloud, Inc. schedules and runs product demos. Sales owns the demo pipeline, and demos are delivered by Solutions Engineering within the Sales organization.

## Intake and Scheduling

Demo requests arrive through the website form, from inbound sales emails, or via an account executive. Every request is logged in the CRM with the prospect's company, team size, use case, and any timeline they mention. A Solutions Engineer reaches out to book a slot within two business days of the request. Standard demos are scheduled for the following week based on mutual availability; prospects with an active procurement deadline are prioritized and can usually be accommodated within 48 hours.

## What Happens on a Demo

A standard demo is a 30-minute live session over video conference. It opens with a short discovery conversation to confirm the prospect's goals, followed by a tailored walkthrough of the workflows that matter to them — typically ticket intake, automation, and reporting. For technical evaluations, the Solutions Engineer covers SSO and SCIM setup, API rate limits, and data residency options relevant to Enterprise buyers. Demos are customized to the prospect's stated use case rather than delivered from a fixed script.

## Who Runs Them

Solutions Engineers run all demos. The owning account executive attends to manage commercial questions, while the Solutions Engineer handles product depth and technical objections. For large or strategic opportunities, a second Solutions Engineer may join to cover specialized topics such as security or migration.

## Follow-Up

Within one business day of the demo, the account executive sends a recap email with a session summary, answers to any open questions, relevant documentation links, and suggested next steps. If the prospect requested pricing, a formal quote follows separately and is valid for 30 days. A 14-day free trial is offered so the team can evaluate hands-on after the session.

## Ownership

Sales owns the demo pipeline; Solutions Engineering delivers the sessions.
